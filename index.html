Anastasios Kontogiorgis

I am a data scientist and artificial intelligence researcher specialising in multi-agent reinforcement learning. I have over seven years of experience across marketing, e-commerce, and fraud risk analysis, combining industry application with academic research through a PhD from Trinity College Dublin (submitted May 2026).

My doctoral research focuses on channel-robust emergent communication, specifically mitigating channel distortions via reliability-aware graph attention layers. This novel framework was advanced during a research internship at the University of Oslo's Department of Informatics, supervised by Prof. Eric Jul. There I developed an autoencoder-based mechanism for estimating communication channel reliability, and co-authored a systematic taxonomy of noise in multi-agent reinforcement learning, currently under review at JAAMAS.

My work emphasises methodological rigor: identifying baseline failure modes, diagnosing dataset biases, and measuring performance against grounded evaluation protocols.

Semantic Book Recommender

An end-to-end redesign of a content-based book recommender. Diagnostic analysis of an earlier iteration uncovered a silent pandas indexing fault that misaligned title vectors across 99.9% of the 236,280-book catalog, leaving only 1 in roughly 989 unaffected. The modernised pipeline enriches 15,000 records via the Open Library API, replacing lexical TF-IDF matching with dense MiniLM sentence embeddings and FAISS vector indexing. This architecture increased subject-relevance retrieval 2.3-fold over the lexical baseline and 14.6-fold over an empirical random floor..

Campaign Uplift & Causal Inference

Observational pre-post comparisons often confuse baseline market trends with true campaign impact. This project isolates causal uplift by constructing synthetic controls via structural time-series models, validated against GBDT and autoregressive baselines. Synthetic stress-testing verified the model's accuracy (+4.62% estimated vs. +4.21% ground truth), against naïve before-and-after and year-on-year comparisons reporting +44.8% and +43.7% respectively. Applied to live retail data, placebo tests exposed treatment selection bias, allowing the impact to be rigorously framed as an operational upper bound.

UAV Emergent-Communication Swarm

An active extension of my doctoral NoisyGraph framework, evaluating whether a channel-robust emergent-communication architecture can generalise from the constrained geometry of an intersection to a mobile swarm. The UAV swarm must cover an unknown area while maintaining link topology under unreliable communication conditions. In the current phase, four seeded runs demonstrate that the architecture does not transfer unchanged. While a staged reward curriculum improves swarm dispersion to a 73.2% coverage peak, performance collapses late in training. Diagnostic analysis isolated two root causes: unconstrained policy updates and a checkpointing criterion that selected for unstable performance spikes over balanced trajectories, each pointing to a specific architectural change for the next phase.

Ethereum Fraud & Data Forensic Analytics

An exploratory and statistical analysis investigating address behaviour on the Ethereum blockchain using non-parametric hypothesis testing and multivariable logistic regression. Data forensic checks identified two prominent label-leakage artefacts—accounting for 77% of target labels—where structural missingness (e.g., absent ERC-20 transfer logs) directly correlated with fraud class assignments. The resulting documentation establishes corrected baselines for predictive modelling on public benchmark data.

Production Phishing Detection Service

A containerised FastAPI service for phishing classification, built alongside rigorous evaluation protocols. Deduplicating the training corpus prior to splitting eliminated a data leakage path that artificially inflated accuracy by 0.5%. Inspecting feature coefficients revealed that legitimate emails were partly identified by historical provenance; when evaluated against a contemporary test set, accuracy dropped from 0.980 to 0.833 while class separation contracted by two-thirds. The deployed service returns calibrated probabilities for flexible thresholding and logs privacy-preserving prediction metrics to detect real-world drift.

Research

My doctoral research addresses a real-world challenge in multi-agent systems: maintaining coordinated behaviour when communication channels are noisy or lossy. I developed NoisyGraph, a framework integrating geometry-driven network topology, autoencoder-based message reliability scoring within graph attention layers, and post-training channel calibration. Evaluated on connected autonomous vehicle environments, the architecture sustains coordination as the channel degrades. Whether that behaviour transfers to a mobile topology the agents must maintain themselves is the open question the UAV continuation investigates. This work comprises two peer-reviewed publications and a comprehensive survey under review at JAAMAS.
